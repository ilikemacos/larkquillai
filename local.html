LarkquillAI Local

Private, offline LarkquillAI that runs on your own computer. No account and no internet needed, and nothing you type leaves your device. Works with Ollama, LM Studio and llama.cpp.

Local 4B

Light and quick. Fits most laptops.

Download 4B
  • 2.7 GB download
  • 8 GB RAM computer

macOS / Linux (Terminal, needs Ollama)

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilikemacos/larkquillai/models/install/install.sh | sh -s -- 4b

Windows (PowerShell, needs Ollama)

$env:LARKQUILL_SIZE='4b'; irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ilikemacos/larkquillai/models/install/install.ps1 | iex

After installing

ollama run larkquill-local:4b

Use larkquill-local:8b for the 8B. Add --think=false for faster, direct answers.

Downloading by hand?

The models come in parts because GitHub allows 2 GB per file. Download every part of one model, then join them: cat file.part1 file.part2 > file.gguf on macOS/Linux or copy /b file.part1 + file.part2 file.gguf on Windows. Then open the .gguf in LM Studio. Checksums and full steps are in the model guide.

LarkquillAI Local runs small open models on your computer, so answers are simpler than the online chat and it can't browse the web.
